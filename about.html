Twelve years of keeping Accra working.

From a two-desk repair counter to the team behind more than 300 offices, schools and shops.

Who we are

BrightTech Solutions started in 2014 as a two-desk laptop repair counter near Kwame Nkrumah Circle. Customers kept asking the same question: if you can fix it, can you also supply it, set it up and connect it to everything else? So we learned to.

Today we are a team of technicians, network engineers and support staff working from our showroom and workshop on Spintex Road. We look after offices, schools, clinics, churches and shops across Greater Accra, and we still repair laptops at the front counter.

Our history

  1. 2014 Opened a two-desk laptop repair counter near Kwame Nkrumah Circle.
  2. 2016 Started supplying new laptops, desktops and printers to small businesses.
  3. 2018 Built our first full office network and CCTV system, for a school in Tema.
  4. 2021 Moved into our own showroom and workshop on Spintex Road.
  5. 2024 Launched the help desk and monthly support plans.
  6. 2026 Looking after more than 300 offices, schools and shops in Greater Accra.

Our mission

To give every business we serve technology that works, and a team that answers when it doesn't.

Our vision

To be the first IT partner Ghanaian businesses call, from the first laptop to the last network cable.
