It started with a friend's laptop.

Four classmates in East Legon, fixing laptops for friends. Then the friends' offices started calling.

How it started

BrightTech Solutions started in 2023 with four classmates in East Legon and one friend's broken laptop. We fixed it. Then we fixed the next one, and soon people we had never met were bringing theirs.

The question kept changing: if you can fix it, can you also supply it, set it up and connect it to everything else? So we learned to. Today the same four of us sell, install and support computers, software and networks for offices, schools, clinics, churches and shops across Greater Accra, and we still fix laptops for friends.

Our history

  1. 2023 Four classmates in East Legon start fixing friends' laptops.
  2. 2024 Friends' offices start calling, and we begin supplying laptops, desktops and printers.
  3. 2025 Built our first full office network and CCTV system, for a school in Tema.
  4. 2026 Opened our showroom and workshop in East Legon, with a help desk and monthly support plans.
  5. Today Looking after more than 40 offices, schools and shops in Greater Accra.

Our mission

To give every business we serve technology that works, and a team that answers when it doesn't.

Our vision

To be the first IT partner Ghanaian businesses call, from the first laptop to the last network cable.
