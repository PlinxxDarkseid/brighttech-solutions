Quick answers

Do you repair computers you didn't sell?

Yes. We repair any brand of laptop, desktop or printer, and diagnosis is free if you go ahead with the repair.

Mobile Money, bank transfer, card or cash at the showroom. Businesses on a support plan can pay monthly by invoice.
